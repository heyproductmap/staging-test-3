# Product-Market Fit — Overview

> Phase 1 — Strategy

Product-market fit work validates that a product solves a real problem for a real market segment with enough demand to sustain growth. It combines quantitative signals (PMF surveys, retention curves) with qualitative insights (positioning, competitive context) to confirm or challenge product direction.
