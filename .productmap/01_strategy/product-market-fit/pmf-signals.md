# PMF Signals

> Phase 1 — Strategy

How to tell whether you are approaching product-market fit — through leading indicators before launch and hard measures after. Update this file after every survey or cohort review; the verdict at the bottom should never be stale.

## Sean Ellis Survey (post-launch)

Ask active users: **"How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?"** Survey only users who recently experienced the core value (e.g., used the product 2+ times in the last two weeks). Threshold: **≥ 40% "very disappointed"** is the classic PMF signal.

## Retention Curve Check (post-launch)

Plot the % of each signup cohort still active week by week. **A curve that flattens** — at any level — means some group gets durable value; a curve that decays toward zero means no fit yet.
