# Business Model — Overview

> Phase 1 — Strategy

Business model work defines how your startup creates, delivers, and captures value. Pre-PMF, the priority is not a polished business plan but a one-page snapshot of your riskiest assumptions — that is what the Lean Canvas is for. Fill it in, mark each block as validated or assumed, and update it after every batch of interviews and experiments.
