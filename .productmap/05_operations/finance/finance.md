# Finance — Overview

> Phase 5 — Operations

Finance operations keep the startup solvent and legible: money flows through proper accounts, the books close every month, and founders always know the current burn and runway. Get the basics right early — cleaning up later is far more expensive than doing it correctly from day one.

## Monthly Close Rhythm

A lightweight monthly close keeps numbers trustworthy for decisions and investor updates.

1. Reconcile bank and card transactions — by [day of month].
2. Categorise expenses; flag anything unusual.
3. Update [runway-burn.md](./runway-burn.md) with actual cash in / cash out.
4. Compare actuals vs. [financial-model.md](./financial-model.md); note variances.
5. Pull headline numbers into the monthly investor update — see [00_company/fundraising/](../../00_company/fundraising/investor-pipeline.md) and [fundraising-strategy.md](../../00_company/fundraising/fundraising-strategy.md).
