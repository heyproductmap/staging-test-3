# Runway & Burn

> Phase 5 — Operations

Track how much cash you spend each month and how long it lasts. Runway is the single number that dictates fundraising timing, hiring pace, and how many experiments you can afford to run.

## Definitions

- **Gross burn:** total cash out per month (all expenses).
- **Net burn:** cash out minus cash in per month. This is what shortens runway.
- **Runway:** current cash balance ÷ average net burn = months remaining.
