## Stage Definitions

| Status | Means |
|--------|-------|
| Research | Identified, checking stage/thesis fit — not yet contacted |
| Intro requested | Asked a mutual connection for the intro |
| First meeting | Intro made or meeting booked/held |
| In diligence | Follow-up questions, data room shared, partner meeting |
| Soft-circled | Verbal yes with amount — not real until signed and wired |
| Committed | Docs signed / money wired |
| Passed | Said no — log the stated reason |
| Parked | Not now — revisit next round |

## Follow-up Cadence

- **After every meeting:** same-day thank-you with the one thing they asked for.
- **No reply:** nudge at day 3–4, once more at day 10, then mark Parked. Two nudges max — chase momentum, not individuals.
- **Investor updates:** monthly email to everyone from First meeting onward — 3 wins, 1 metric, 1 ask. Passed investors who opted in stay on the list; they fund the next round.
- **Momentum rule:** batch first meetings into a 2–3 week window so partner conversations and term sheets land close together.
