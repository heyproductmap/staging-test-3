# Pitch Deck Outline

> Slide-by-slide outline for the current fundraise. Draft the narrative here, then build the visual deck. One idea per slide — if a slide needs a paragraph, it's two slides.

---

## Slide Outline

### 1. Title

One line: company name + what you do in plain words. [e.g. "Acme — expense reports that file themselves."]

> [Fill in: your one-liner — reuse from [onepager.md](../product/onepager.md)]

### 2. Problem

Who has the problem, how painful it is, and what it costs them today. Make it concrete — one vivid example beats three abstractions.

> [Fill in: problem statement + evidence from customer conversations]

### 3. Solution

What you built (or are building) and how it kills the problem. Show the product, don't describe the category.

> [Fill in: solution in 2–3 bullets + screenshot placeholder]

### 4. Why Now

The structural shift (tech, regulation, behaviour) that makes this possible today and impossible five years ago.

> [Fill in: 1–2 why-now drivers]

### 5. Market

Bottom-up sizing: who exactly, how many, what they'd pay. A credible $500M beats a hand-wavy $50B.

> [Fill in: TAM/SAM/SOM with your math — link segmentation: [../../01_strategy/segmentation/segmentation.md](../../01_strategy/segmentation/segmentation.md)]

### 6. Product

How it works in 2–3 steps, from the user's point of view. Demo or screenshots over architecture.

> [Fill in: core flow + what's live vs. planned]

### 7. Traction

Whatever is real: revenue, users, retention, waitlist, LOIs, pilot results. Show the trend, not just the total.

> [Fill in: your strongest 2–3 numbers with time axis]

### 8. Business Model

Who pays, how much, and the shape of unit economics. One pricing slide — not the whole pricing page.

> [Fill in: pricing + early CAC/LTV signal — see [../../03_analysis/unit-economics/unit-economics.md](../../03_analysis/unit-economics/unit-economics.md)]

### 9. Competition

Honest landscape and your durable edge. Investors know the alternatives — naming them builds trust.

> [Fill in: positioning — pull from [competitor-matrix.md](../../01_strategy/competitors/competitor-matrix.md) and [value-proposition.md](../product/value-proposition.md)]

### 10. Team

Why *this* team wins *this* market: relevant earned insight, not full CVs.

> [Fill in: founders + one line each on unfair advantage — see [team.md](../team.md)]

### 11. Ask & Use of Funds

How much, what it buys, and the milestones it unlocks. Must match [fundraising-strategy.md](./fundraising-strategy.md) exactly.

> [Fill in: raise amount + 3 milestone bullets]

### 12. Vision (optional closer)

Where this goes if it works — the big version of the company, in one memorable line.

> [Fill in: closing line — reuse from [product-vision.md](../product/product-vision.md)]
