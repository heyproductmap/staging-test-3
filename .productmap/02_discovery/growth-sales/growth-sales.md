# Growth & Sales — Overview

> Phase 2 — Discovery

Before PMF there is no growth machine — there are founders selling. Early growth and sales work is about closing the first customers by hand, learning why they buy (or don't), and only then systematising what worked into repeatable channels and loops.

## Early-Stage Focus

- **Sell before you scale:** every early deal is a discovery interview with revenue attached.
- **Track a simple pipeline:** stages and counts live in [founder-led-sales.md](./founder-led-sales.md).
- **Treat channels as experiments:** log channel bets in [experiments.md](../experiments/experiments.md) with explicit success criteria.

## Required Skills

- Product Marketing
- Fluency with Data
- Business Goals Ownership
