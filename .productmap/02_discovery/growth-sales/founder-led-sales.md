## Discovery Call Structure (30 min)

1. **Context (5 min)** — their role, team, current workflow. No pitching yet.
2. **Problem (10 min)** — Mom Test questions from the [interview guide](../user-research/interview-guide.md): last occurrence, cost, what they tried.
3. **Fit check (5 min)** — do they match the ICP qualification criteria? If not, end warmly and ask for referrals.
4. **Show, then ask (7 min)** — short demo mapped to *their* stated problem only.
5. **Commitment (3 min)** — concrete next step with a date: pilot, second call with the buyer, or proposal.

## Pricing Conversations

- Name a price and hold the silence; discounting on reflex destroys the willingness-to-pay signal.
- Trade, don't discount: lower price only for a commitment (case study, annual prepay, intros).
