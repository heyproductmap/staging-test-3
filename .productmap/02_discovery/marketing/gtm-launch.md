## Launch Checklist

**Pre-launch**

- [ ] Positioning statement tested on 5+ ICP conversations
- [ ] Landing page live with a working CTA and analytics events
- [ ] Onboarding path walked end-to-end by someone outside the team
- [ ] Launch assets ready: announcement post, demo, screenshots
- [ ] Waitlist / early users warmed up and asked to support the launch

**Launch day**

- [ ] Announcement out on chosen channels; founder replies to every comment
- [ ] Analytics and error monitoring watched live
- [ ] Personal outreach to the first signups within hours

**Post-launch (first 2 weeks)**

- [ ] Interview 5+ new signups — why they came, what they expected ([interview guide](../user-research/interview-guide.md))
- [ ] Channel results logged against hypotheses above
- [ ] Retention of the launch cohort tracked in [analytics](../../03_analysis/analytics/analytics.md)
