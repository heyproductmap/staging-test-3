# Marketing — Overview

> Phase 2 — Discovery

Early-stage marketing is not brand-building — it is finding the words and the one or two channels that make your beachhead segment respond. Positioning and messaging come straight from discovery interviews (use your customers' own language); channels are experiments to run and kill, not commitments.

## Early-Stage Focus

- **Positioning:** who it is for, what category it belongs to, and why it beats the current alternative — anchored to your [ICP](../../01_strategy/segmentation/segmentation.md).
- **Messaging:** phrases pulled verbatim from [discovery interviews](../user-research/interview-guide.md), tested on landing pages before ad spend.
- **First channels:** pick 1–2 where your ICP already gathers; treat each as a hypothesis in [experiments.md](../experiments/experiments.md).
