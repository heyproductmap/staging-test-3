# User Research — Overview

> Phase 2 — Discovery

User research work generates the qualitative and quantitative evidence needed to make confident product decisions. It spans planning through synthesis, producing structured artefacts that capture what users do, say, think, and feel so the team can act on real insight rather than assumption.

---

## Key Artefacts

| Artefact | Description |
|---|---|
| [problem-framing.md](./problem-framing.md) | Problem framing techniques to sharpen the right question |
| research-plan.md | Research plan with questions, methodology, and timeline (not yet created) |
| [interview-guide.md](./interview-guide.md) | Customer discovery interview guide with Mom Test principles and question structure |
| usability-test-plan.md | Usability test plan with tasks, metrics, and analysis approach (not yet created) |
| synthesis-report.md | Research synthesis report with themes, evidence, and recommendations (not yet created) |

- Voice of Customer
- User Experience
- Product Discovery
