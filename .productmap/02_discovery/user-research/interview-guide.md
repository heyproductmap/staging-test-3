# Customer Discovery Interview Guide

> Phase 2 — Discovery

A guide for founder-led discovery interviews, built on Mom Test principles: talk about the customer's life, not your idea. The goal is evidence about real behaviour, not compliments about your product.

---

## Ground Rules (Mom Test)

- **Past behaviour, not hypotheticals.** Ask "When did you last...?" and "Walk me through the last time...", never "Would you use...?"
- **No pitching.** If you are explaining your solution, you are no longer learning. Save the demo for a separate call.
- **Facts and commitments over opinions.** Compliments are noise. Real signal is time, money, or reputation: an intro, a follow-up meeting, a presale.
- **Dig for specifics.** "It's annoying" is not data. "I spent 3 hours last Tuesday and still missed the deadline" is.

## 30-Minute Structure

| Time | Section | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| 0–3 min | Intro | Set context: learning, not selling. Ask permission to record. |
| 3–10 min | Problem exploration | Their role, workflow, and where [problem area] shows up. |
| 10–18 min | Current solutions | What they do today, what it costs them, what they have already tried or paid for. |
| 18–25 min | Dig into specifics | The most recent concrete episode: walk through it step by step. |
| 25–30 min | Commitment / next step | Ask for something that costs them something (see below). |
